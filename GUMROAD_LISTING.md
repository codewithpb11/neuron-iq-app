## Short description

Train reasoning, memory, strategy, and pattern skills with a 30-question practice assessment, a tailored game plan, and eight focused brain games for Android.

## Full description

Neuron IQ is a privacy-first Android brain-training app built for short, focused practice sessions.

Start with an untimed 30-question reasoning assessment. It records the time you take, weighs question difficulty, and creates a **practice estimate** to recommend starting levels across the app.

Then train the skills you want to sharpen with eight games:

- Word Connect
- Chess with ten bot levels, including local Stockfish-powered challenges
- Connect 4
- Memory Match
- 2048
- Tic-Tac-Toe
- Jigsaw Puzzle with five image choices
- Sudoku

### Early Access notice

This is an Early Access release. The app is functional, but gameplay, balancing, design, and features may change with future updates. The supplied APK is permanently release-signed for direct Android installation and is not a Google Play build.

### Assessment notice

The in-app score is a practice estimate for learning and entertainment—not a clinical IQ test. It is not for medical, educational, employment, or diagnostic use. The app does not promise to increase IQ or intelligence.

### Compatibility

Android 7.0 (Nougat) or newer. Internet is not required for normal in-app use after installation.

### Open source

The chess engine includes Stockfish.js 19 Lite under GPLv3. The purchase includes source materials and the applicable licence notice.
